\begin{column}{\colwidth}

  \begin{block}{Abstract}
    Large language models produce confident but factually incorrect outputs
    at rates that span three to ninety-one percent across deployment
    domains, a failure mode that increased parameter count alone does not
    close. This thesis proposes Confidence-Aware Episodic Memory with
    Self-Improvement (CAEM), an architectural intervention that treats
    hallucination reduction as a system-level problem rather than a
    post-hoc detection problem. A single query passes through a verified
    episodic memory store, a confidence-aware three-tier router with an
    independent safety override and a retrieval-utility classifier, and a
    nine-signal verifier ensemble spanning internal calibration,
    sample-set agreement, external grounding, and question-answer
    relevance, whose per-benchmark calibrated-probability composite gates
    storage at two fixed cut-points held constant across cycles.
    Self-improvement is delivered through low-rank adaptation over a
    frozen Qwen-2.5-3B-Instruct backbone, with every cycle boundary
    refitting calibration on a fresh fold under a multi-modal retention
    guard whose asymmetric rollback reverts the adapter while preserving
    memory. Against the matched zero-shot
    baseline on the same backbone, on a five-benchmark panel split into
    training (FEVER, TriviaQA, CommonsenseQA) and transfer (TruthfulQA,
    StrategyQA), the within-trajectory best cycle reaches a pooled
    exact-match gain of $+20.3\%$ and a pooled composite hallucination
    metric reduction of $-29.4\%$ on the training panel; against the
    locked external eight-baseline panel, CAEM leads every baseline on
    pooled composite hallucination at both cycles and on pooled exact
    match at the best cycle, reaching exact-match parity with the
    strongest baselines at trajectory end.
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Research Objective}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Verified episodic memory with a four-outcome decision}
        whose admission rule is finer than a single binary threshold.
      \item \textbf{Pre-generation confidence, three-tier routing with
        safety override, and a retrieval-utility classifier} computed
        from a single short forward pass and calibrated via per-benchmark
        temperature scaling on a disjoint fold.
      \item \textbf{Nine-signal post-generation verifier with a calibrated
        probability composite} across four families: internal calibration,
        sample-set agreement, external grounding under three aggregation
        rules, and question-answer relevance.
      \item \textbf{Fixed-threshold storage gate with two registered
        cut-points} held constant across cycles and one abstention floor.
      \item \textbf{Retroactive review and deferred reconsideration at
        cycle boundaries} that rescore stored episodes under the updated
        model and promote deferred entries past threshold.
      \item \textbf{Constrained self-improvement with a parameter-efficient
        adapter and a multi-modal retention guard} that aborts the cycle
        with an adapter rollback when the worst probe ratio falls below a
        fixed floor.
      \item \textbf{Empirical validation against eight external baselines
        and pre-registered ablations} on a five-benchmark panel under
        matched protocol.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Methodology}
    The architecture organises a single query into an eight-stage pipeline
    traversed once per query and wrapped in a per-cycle update loop.
    Stage 1 produces a pre-routing confidence from a single short forward
    pass. Stage 2 encodes the query and probes a similarity-indexed
    episodic memory for the nearest stored entry. Stage 3 combines the
    similarity and stored-quality scores into a dispatch score and routes
    to one of three tiers, with a safety override on low confidence.
    Stage 3b, a retrieval-utility classifier, refines the dispatch into
    zero-shot or retrieval-augmented tier per query. Stage 4 executes the
    chosen tier; Stage 5 passes the answer through a nine-signal
    post-generation verifier; Stage 6 applies a four-outcome decision
    (store, defer, abstain, discard); Stage 7 returns the user-facing
    action. Stage 8, at cycle boundaries, runs retroactive re-verification,
    deferred reconsideration, memory consolidation, and a regularised
    fine-tune under a retention guard with asymmetric rollback: weights
    are rolled back on retention failure, but memory is preserved.
  \end{block}

\end{column}

  \begin{block}{Model Architecture}
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.95\colwidth,keepaspectratio]{figures/architecture_source.pdf}

    \vspace{0.3em}
    \footnotesize
    The eight-stage pipeline routes each query through pre-routing
    confidence, memory lookup, three-tier dispatch with the Stage 3b
    retrieval-utility classifier ($\tau_{\text{RUC}} = 0.375$), tier
    execution, nine-signal verification, the four-outcome decision under
    fixed cut-points $\tau_{\text{store}} = 0.60$ / $\tau_{\text{defer}} = 0.45$,
    and output; Stage 8 closes every cycle boundary with retroverify
    ($\tau_{\text{retro}} = 0.50$), deferred reconsider (TTL $= 2$),
    consolidate, LoRA fine-tune ($r = 32$, $\alpha = 64$, $\tau_{\text{train}} = 0.70$),
    and the multi-modal retention probe under floor $\rho_{\min} = 0.93$
    with asymmetric rollback (adapter reverted, memory preserved).
    Backbone Qwen-2.5-3B-Instruct, frozen across the cycle.
  \end{block}

  \begin{block}{Headline Result}
    Against the matched zero-shot baseline B1 on the same backbone, the
    training-panel architectural-contribution slice at the within-trajectory
    best cycle (C2) reaches a pooled exact-match gain of $+20.3\%$ relative
    and a pooled composite hallucination metric reduction of $-29.4\%$
    relative; the trajectory-end cycle holds at $+13.7\%$ EM and $-20.3\%$
    CHM. CAEM beats every external baseline on composite hallucination at
    both reference cycles and on exact match at the best cycle, reaching
    exact-match parity with the strongest baselines at trajectory end.

    \[
      \boxed{\Delta\mathrm{EM} = +20.3\%\ \text{rel.}} \qquad
      \boxed{\Delta\mathrm{CHM} = -29.4\%\ \text{rel.}}
    \]
    \centering\footnotesize
    (training panel, CAEM C2 vs.\ B1 zero-shot baseline)
