Aksan (2026-10-16): bare numbered citations like "Confabulations, in the sense of [70]" force the reader to click to learn whose study it is. He approved the rule below for the whole thesis.

**Rule:**
- Sentence relies on a specific study's finding, number or method → name it: "Guo et al.~\cite{..} found ...".
- General background statement → bare citation at sentence end is fine.
- Never use the bracket as a noun ("in the sense of \cite", "the approach of \cite", "introduced by \cite").
- Write "et al.", not "and colleagues".
- Recap sections (ch2 Summary of Key Findings) may stay bare; studies were already named.
