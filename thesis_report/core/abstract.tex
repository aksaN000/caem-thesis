\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Large language models produce confident but factually incorrect outputs at rates that span three to ninety-one percent across deployment domains \cite{alkaissi2023artificial,10.1145/3649506}, a failure mode that scaling parameter count does not close \cite{lin-etal-2022-truthfulqa}. This thesis proposes \emph{Confidence-Aware Episodic Memory with Self-Improvement (CAEM)}, an architectural intervention that treats hallucination reduction as a system-level problem rather than a post-hoc detection problem. CAEM organises a single query into an eight-stage pipeline that combines a verified episodic memory store, a confidence-aware three-tier router with an independent safety override, a nine-signal verifier ensemble across four families (internal calibration, sample-set agreement, external grounding, and question-answer relevance), and a per-benchmark calibrated-probability composite that fits per-signal isotonic regression on each training-panel slice, blends every per-benchmark curve toward a pooled prior through a James-Stein style shrinkage at strength $\alpha = 0.6$, and aggregates the resulting probabilities through a regularised logistic boost layer \cite{cherian2024conformal}. The composite output feeds a fixed-threshold storage gate at the two registered cut-points $\tau_{\text{store}} = 0.60$ and $\tau_{\text{defer}} = 0.45$, held constant across cycles, with the cycle-boundary refit absorbing distribution drift into the signal-to-probability mapping rather than into the threshold itself; the design replaced an earlier split-conformal alternative \cite{mohri2024conformal} that was rejected on the registered calibration-to-evaluation distribution-shift evidence. The architecture is wrapped in a cycle-boundary self-improvement loop that fine-tunes the generator through a low-rank adaptation layer at rank thirty-two and scaling factor sixty-four over the attention and feed-forward projections, with the underlying backbone frozen and the bounded adapter parameter budget acting as the implicit cycle-to-cycle drift bound in the spirit of elastic weight consolidation \cite{hu2022lora,doi:10.1073/pnas.1611835114}. After every fine-tune the loop refits the temperature scalar and per-signal isotonic curves and per-benchmark boost weights on a fresh calibration fold drawn from the post-fine-tune signal distribution, reloads the verifier, and runs retroactive re-verification against the freshly recalibrated composite before the next cycle begins. The empirical programme uses the Qwen-2.5-3B-Instruct backbone on a five-benchmark panel split into a training panel of three (FEVER, TriviaQA, CommonsenseQA) and a held-out transfer panel of two (TruthfulQA, StrategyQA). Cycle zero produces a locked configuration with realised calibration-fold storage precision approximately $0.64$ over $478$ stored entries at the registered cut-point $\tau_{\text{store}} = 0.60$, drawn from a twenty-five-variant pre-registered sweep over the storage cut-point and the boost layer regularisation strength.

The architectural contribution is paired with a theoretical contribution: four theorems characterise the trajectory of the storage class across cycles. Pool purity is bounded below by the calibration-anchored empirical precision under upward-only retroactive update; storage rate and pool purity are monotonic in the verifier's discrimination and the base-model accuracy; the precision-asymptote gap is bounded by a geometric envelope under a contraction rate set by the per-cycle storage rate and the calibration-fold Cohen's $d$; and the asymptotic hallucination rate of the memory-direct tier is bounded by a structural floor set by the registered cut-point's calibration-fold precision rather than by deployment scale. Each theorem is paired with an empirical receipt evaluated against the realised cycle horizon, and every threshold, fit-time procedure, and artefact path is committed in code before the empirical record is consulted. The architecture's value over the simpler baseline of plain exact-match-labelled fine-tuning at the same labelling budget rests on five load-bearing differentiators: a per-query precision contract that fine-tuning structurally cannot produce, a memory-direct serving tier that compounds cost savings as the verified pool accumulates, an asymmetric-rollback property under which a failed cycle preserves accumulated memory while reverting the adapter, a verifier-as-sample-efficiency-multiplier under which the cycle-boundary labelled batch validates a precision contract rather than carrying the gradient signal directly, and inference-time error catching that persists after the parametric fine-tune saturates against the generator's capacity ceiling. The ten-cycle trajectory, the external-baseline panel under matched protocol with paired McNemar significance and bias-corrected-and-accelerated bootstrap intervals under Holm correction, and the architectural ablation panel against pre-registered effect bands are reported in the empirical chapter; the artefact catalogue, snapshot recovery recipe, and version-pinned reproducibility envelope are registered in the appendices.
