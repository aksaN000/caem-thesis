# Reference check 2: do the cited papers support the thesis claims?

This was checked against the **full text of each paper**. The PDFs were downloaded to the *CAEM refs* Google Drive folder, converted to text and searched. Reference numbers are the printed thesis numbers.

For each reference, every place the thesis cites it is listed with the **full sentence** (or table row) carrying the citation, next to the **exact passage from the paper** that confirms or contradicts it, with its page.

**How the quotes were verified:** every quotation in the "What the paper says" column was machine-checked to occur word-for-word in the cited PDF's text (ignoring line breaks and hyphenation). Exceptions:
- quotes taken from a *suggested replacement* paper are labelled as such;
- table values are reported as numbers read from the named table;
- quotes from the scanned papers [31], [87], [92] and [93] match the OCR text at 96–99%; the differences are OCR errors only;
- text marked *Preliminary* comes from an abstract only, because the full PDF was not available.

**Verdicts**
- ✅ **Matched**: the paper says this. Citations that only attribute a method, model or dataset count as matched when the attribution is correct.
- ⚠️ **Partly**: part is right, but a number, detail or attribution is wrong or overstated (the *Why* column says what).
- ❌ **Not matched**: the paper does not say this, or says the opposite.
- 🔒 **PDF needed**: the PDF could not be obtained, so the claim is not yet checked.

**% matched** = ✅ ÷ citations checked against a PDF (🔒 rows excluded).
