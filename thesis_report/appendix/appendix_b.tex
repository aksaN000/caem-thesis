\section{The long-hypothesis judge ablation diagnostic}
\label{app:judge-ablation}

The Platt fit diagnostic that licensed the ablation of the long-hypothesis judge is recorded in \verb|outputs/calibration/qwen_judge_platt.ablation.json|. The headline numbers are: $n = 500$ overlap fold, Platt slope $0.137$, Platt intercept $1.247$, Pearson $\rho = 0.5843$ in logit space against a pre-registered floor of $0.70$, mean absolute error in logit space $1.408$, and mean absolute error in probability space $0.230$. The verdict is therefore that the long-hypothesis judge fails the registered correlation floor; \Cref{sec:adj-judge-ablation} reports the architectural consequence and the future-work item that would re-fit the judge under task-specific training.
