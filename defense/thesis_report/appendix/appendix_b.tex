\section{The long-hypothesis judge ablation diagnostic}
\label{app:judge-ablation}

The Platt fit diagnostic that licensed the ablation of the long-hypothesis judge is recorded in the long-hypothesis-judge Platt-ablation calibration record. The two persisted headline statistics are the observed Pearson $\rho = 0.5843$ in logit space and the pre-specified floor of $\rho_{\min} = 0.70$ against which the observed correlation is audited. The judge therefore failed the floor and was dropped, so every hypothesis is judged by MiniCheck with inputs truncated at $512$ tokens (\Cref{sec:adj-judge-ablation}); revisiting it is future work (\Cref{sec:future-judge}). The per-sample trace is kept in the judge's diagnostic log.
