\section{The $100$-variant composite parameter sweep}
\label{app:sweep-full}

The locked configuration selected from the sweep, with the rationale for each parameter, is given in \Cref{tab:sweep-top}; the locked variant and its neighbours are compared in \Cref{tab:sweep-variants}. The full sweep over the pre-specified $\tau_{\text{store}} \times C \times \alpha_{\text{shrink}}$ grid ($4 \times 5 \times 5 = 100$ variants) is recorded in the cycle-zero sweep archive with one record per variant; no per-variant comparison table is reproduced in the thesis. Per-benchmark sweep diagnostics for any variant in the grid are recoverable by reading the corresponding JSON entry.
